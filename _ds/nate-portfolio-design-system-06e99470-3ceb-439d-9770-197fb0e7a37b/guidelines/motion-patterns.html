Scroll reveal & hover elevation

Two shared patterns. Both live in tokens/motion.css; the reveal is driven by motion/scroll-reveal.js.

1 · Scroll reveal

Section eyebrows, headings and subtitles fade up once when they first enter the viewport. They do not reset on the way back up, and they never animate under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce. Body paragraphs, cards, photography and the hero stay static.

TokenValueUsed for
--reveal-distance20pxInitial translateY
--reveal-duration550msOpacity + transform
--reveal-easeease-outBoth properties
--reveal-stagger90msDelay per sibling in the section

Markup: mark the page container data-reveal-root. Every section / .about-section inside it staggers its own heading children in document order. Opt one element in with data-reveal, out with data-reveal="off". After client-side routing, call window.NateScrollReveal.refresh().

2 · Hover elevation

The lift PhotoCard already used, extended to the solid GlassCard: translateY(-3px) plus --shadow-hover over .22s ease. Only transform and shadow move — fill, border and radius hold. Suppressed on hover: none pointers, where a sticky hover state would read as a stuck card.
