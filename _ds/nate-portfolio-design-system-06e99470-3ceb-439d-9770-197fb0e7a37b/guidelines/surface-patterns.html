Corner glow, work band, card date, full-bleed close

Patterns added after the original import, all in patterns/surfaces.css.

1 · GlassCard corner glow

The yellow glow moved out of the section background and onto the card: a radial gradient in the top-left corner layered over --card. One glow per card. Sections are flat — .section-surface carries no gradient. Photo cards never take it.

Problem
Glow at 16% in light mode.
Insight
10% in dark mode, same geometry.
Impact
Behind content, never over a photo.

2 · Selected work band

2025
Product Design · Veevart

Point-of-sale redesign

Full-bleed container on --bg-foot, dark in both themes, title centered above the cards. It now carries its own top-left corner glow (a section-level sibling of the GlassCard glow, always --glow-dark) and rounds its top corners only (--card-radius) — the bottom stays square because the footer sits flush against it. The gap from the band's top edge to the title equals the gap from the title to the first card (--work-band-title-gap, 40px both places); the bottom padding (--work-band-pad) is separate. The date range that used to sit beside the title is gone — the year is on each card now.

3 · Project-card date

.card-date: top-right, --card-date-size 30px (the same size as the card title), white at 55% (--card-date-color). It reads as scale, not as emphasis, and it is no longer part of the meta row.

4 · Full-bleed next-project card

.card-fullbleed: the closing card breaks the 1400px wrap, runs edge to edge with square corners, and hugs its content height instead of the old fixed 280px. Body padding grows to 56/48 so the text keeps its breathing room at full width. Its :focus-visible ring is drawn inset in bright --yellow so it stays visible when the card sits flush against the dark footer.

5 · Flush dark-section stacking

A utility, not a look: .foot-sec.flush zeros the footer's standard top margin for pages that close on a full-bleed dark section (work band, next-project card), so the two read as one block. The page router toggles the class — pages that close on a bordered content block instead keep the normal gap.
